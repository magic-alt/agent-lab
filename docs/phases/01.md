# Phase 01 — Transformer mechanics

## Goal
Understand attention by executing it, not by memorizing a block diagram.

## Tasks
- Build Q/K/V tensors.
- Compute scaled dot-product attention.
- Apply a causal mask.
- Verify each row sums to 1 after softmax.
- Demonstrate that token i cannot attend to j > i.

## Acceptance
- Offline test validates tensor shapes and causal masking.
- The experiment prints score/weight/output shapes and forbidden future mass.
